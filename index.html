About Me

A decade at the intersection of harmonic & functional analysis

My research lies at the intersection of harmonic analysis and functional analysis, with a particular focus on analysis associated with non-standard function spaces. I bring nearly a decade of university-level teaching experience, an active research career, graduate supervision and academic leadership.
